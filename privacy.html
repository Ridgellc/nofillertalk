Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

No Filler Talk ("we") runs the No Filler Talk YouTube channel and Instagram account and an internal publishing tool, the No Filler Talk Uploader, that uploads our own clips to our own YouTube channel using the YouTube API Services.

Who uses the tool

The No Filler Talk Uploader is used only by the No Filler Talk team to manage our own channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

The tool signs in to our own YouTube channel with Google OAuth and uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to that channel and to set each video's title, description and privacy status. It does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user or viewer.

How data is stored

The OAuth credentials for our channel are stored only on our own computer, are not shared with anyone, and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. We do not sell or share any data.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Viewers of our content

We do not collect personal information from people who watch our clips. YouTube and Instagram may collect data under their own policies.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: nofillertalk@gmail.com.
